Find the five-letter word in six guesses. Green means right letter, right place. Gold means the letter belongs elsewhere. Grey means that copy of the letter is not needed.

After three unsuccessful guesses, you can reveal one meaning-based clue. Your result records whether you used it. There’s no points penalty.

Your Birman reacts to new discoveries. Repeating a known letter won’t earn another celebration.
